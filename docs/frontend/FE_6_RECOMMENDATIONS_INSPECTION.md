## 3. Recommendation Data Model & Lifecycle Progression

### Canonical States (`RecommendationStatus`):
- `ACTIVE`: Generated by deterministic signal engine; armed and awaiting entry condition.
- `TRIGGERED`: Market price penetrated the entry threshold; position is active.
- `TARGET_HIT`: Confirmed market price reached Target 1 or Target 2.
- `STOPPED`: Confirmed market price hit stop loss.
- `EXPIRED`: Session close elapsed for intraday signals (15:30 IST) or validity period expired for delivery.
- `INVALIDATED`: Underlying market or setup conditions invalidated prior to entry.
- `CANCELLED`: Programmatically or manually cancelled.
- `AMBIGUOUS_INTRABAR`: Both stop and target were touched within the same OHLC bar (resolved conservatively).

---

## 4. Outcome Tracking & Performance Metrics

### Measured Outcome Fields (`RecommendationOutcome`):
- **Entry Metrics:** `entry_triggered` (bool), `entry_timestamp` (datetime), `actual_entry_price` (Decimal).
- **Exit Metrics:** `exit_reason` (`TARGET_HIT`, `STOP_HIT`, `EXPIRED_SESSION`, `EXPIRED_MAX_DURATION`, `AMBIGUOUS_INTRABAR`, `NO_ENTRY`), `exit_timestamp`, `actual_exit_price`.
- **P&L & Return:** `net_pnl_percent` (post-cost net %), `gross_pnl_percent`, `net_pnl_absolute` (₹), `cost_absolute` (brokerage + STT + slippage).
- **Risk Attribution:** `r_multiple` (Net P&L / Initial Risk), `risk_amount` (|Entry - Stop|).
- **Excursion Analysis:** `max_favorable_excursion` (MFE %), `max_adverse_excursion` (MAE %).
- **Duration:** `holding_duration_bars`, `holding_duration_seconds`, `bars_evaluated`.

### Performance Aggregations (`GET /api/v1/strategies/{strategy}/performance/`):
- `sample_size`: Total completed trade outcomes evaluated.
- `wins`: Count of `TARGET_HIT` outcomes.
- `losses`: Count of `STOP_HIT` outcomes.
- `win_rate`: Ratio of wins to total sample (`wins / sample_size`).
- `avg_r_multiple`: Mean R-multiple across evaluated trades.
- `is_small_sample`: Boolean flag set to `true` when `sample_size < 30` to prevent statistical overconfidence.

---

## 5. Point-In-Time (PIT) & Lookahead Protection

- All recommendations store immutable `as_of` and `generated_at` timestamps.
- Outcomes are evaluated strictly against chronological market OHLCV bars using `RecommendationOutcomeService` with `select_for_update()` to prevent race conditions.
- Historical recommendations and outcomes are immutable records.
